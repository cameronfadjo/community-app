# Delete your account

You can delete your [app name] account at any time.

## In the app

1. Open the app and tap the account button at the top of the screen.
2. Scroll down and tap **Delete my account**.
3. Confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

## Without the app

Write to [contact email] from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will reply to confirm when it is done.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in
- Your name and email address
- Your record of perks

## What is kept

Totals that do not identify you, such as how many perks were unlocked at an event, are kept.

See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for more.
