# Support

Write to [contact email] and we will get back to you.

## Common questions

### Do I need an account?

No. You can browse events and get directions without one. An account is only needed to unlock perks.

### A perk did not unlock

Perks unlock when you are at the venue, from 30 minutes before the event starts until it ends. Check that the app is allowed to use your location while it is open, then tap the perk again.

### My perk ran out of time

A perk can be shown for 15 minutes after it unlocks. Each perk can be used once per person per event.

### An event's details are wrong

Events are posted by the venues and hosts themselves. Tell us at [contact email] and we will pass it on.

### I run a venue and want to be listed

Listing is free. Write to [contact email].
