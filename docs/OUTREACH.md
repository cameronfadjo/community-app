# Venue outreach plan

How to get the first venues and hosts into the app, starting in New Haven.

The printable materials are in the Venue Outreach Kit:
https://claude.ai/artifact/GJvfhAJ4cza3FJvNhZkxkj

## What is in the kit

| Piece | Size | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| One-pager, front and back | Letter, two sides | The owner or manager |
| Leave-behind card | Half letter | Left when the owner is out |
| Counter sign | Letter | Customers, at venues running a perk |
| Staff card | Half letter | Bartenders and door staff |
| Plan: first eight weeks | Letter, landscape | You |
| Tracker: New Haven leads | Letter, landscape | You |
| Guide: what to say | Letter, landscape | You |
| Plan: the offer | Letter, landscape | You |

Export any piece as a PDF from the kit and print it.

## Fill these in before printing

Anything in square brackets is a blank for you to fill. Nothing has been
made up on your behalf.

| Blank | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| Your name, phone, and email | Every hand-out |
| QR codes: venue sign-up link and app download link | One-pager, card, counter sign |
| Founding venue terms | One-pager, guide, offer |
| Revenue terms for ticket sales | One-pager back |
| Perk pricing after launch | Offer |
| How many founding venues per town | Offer |

Do not quote a price or a share to an owner until it is written down. See
`TICKETING.md` for how the ticket fee could work.

## The pitch in one breath

It is free to be listed. People nearby see your events when they pick what
they are up for. A perk gives them a reason to choose you, and you see how
many people used it. Paid extras come later and are optional.

## What is true today, and what is not

Say only what the app does now.

| You can say | You cannot say yet |
|---|---|
| Listing is free | How many people use the app |
| Venues can change or cancel events themselves | That the app is in the App Store or Google Play |
| Perks unlock only at the venue, one per person per event | That tickets can be sold in the app |
| Venues see totals for each perk, never names | What the ticket fee will be |
| Nobody needs an account to browse | |
| The app is for adults | |

Until the app is in the stores, demo it on your own phone.

## The eight weeks

| Weeks | Focus | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 and 2 | Get ready: print, fill blanks, set up the demo, decide founding terms | You can show the app in two minutes without signal |
| 3 and 4 | Visit the New Haven bars in person | Every bar on the list has had one visit |
| 5 and 6 | Follow up, post confirmed events, meet community hosts | About 15 recurring events are confirmed |
| 7 and 8 | First perks; start Hartford and Norwalk lists | Two venues have run a perk and seen their totals |

Visit bars Tuesday to Thursday, mid-afternoon, before doors open. Ask for
the owner or manager by name. Show the app first and hand over paper second.

## Who to see first

Bars and clubs, because they have the least schedule information in the
directory and the most to gain from a perk:

- Partners Bar & Nightclub
- Gotham Citi Cafe
- 168 York Street Cafe
- Blue Orchid Bar & Restaurant
- Stella Blues

Community hosts, because they run the most recurring events:

- Trans Haven
- New Haven Pride Center
- Possible Futures, Never Ending Books, Elm City Games, Ascent Climbing

Pride groups and centers are listed as hosts of events people can turn up
to. Their services are not listed in the app.

None of these has been contacted. Look up each address and its opening
hours before you visit. Full lists are in `launch-connecticut/`.

## Each week, count

- Venues visited
- Venues listed
- Events confirmed
- Perks live
- Taps on "Take me there"

## Rules to hold to

- No events for people under 18.
- Users are never charged.
- No promise on revenue until the terms are written down.
- Paid placements, when they exist, are labeled as paid.
