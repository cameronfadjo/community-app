# Partner insights

What venues and event hosts can learn from the app, and the lines that are
never crossed. Agreed on September 27, 2026.

## The test for any new number

It must work from totals alone, with no account, phone, or location
attached, and it must not let a host work out who any one person is.

## Safeguards that apply to everything

1. Totals only, with nothing that identifies a person or phone.
2. Day and hour, never exact times.
3. Breakdowns are hidden when the number is under five.
4. Counted once per phone, per event, per day.
5. Hosts see only their own events.
6. No new Cloud Functions.

## The plan

| Stage | What hosts get | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Views, when people looked, asked for directions, and the perk funnel. An Insights page per event and per venue. Perk records deleted after 30 days. | Built |
| 2 | Where views came from, ticket and link taps, how far ahead people look, arrival hours, unused perks | To do |
| 3 | Repeating events over time, and demand with nothing to meet it, by town | To do |
| Later | New versus returning, benchmarks, a weekly summary by email | When there is enough use |

"Asked for directions" is the sign that someone means to go. The app has no
"I'm going" button, by design.

## Stage 2 and 3 ideas

| Question a host would ask | What would be counted |
|---|---|
| Where did my views come from? | Which screen led to the event |
| How often was I shown versus opened? | Times the card appeared, against times it was tapped |
| Which activity brought people in? | Which activity they were browsing |
| What were people looking for? | Which filters were on |
| Did they want tickets? | Taps on the ticket link |
| Did they check us out? | Taps on the website and social links |
| Did they pass it on? | Taps on share |
| How far ahead do people look? | Same day, the day before, or earlier |
| When do people arrive? | Perk unlocks by hour |
| Are perks going unused? | Unlocked but never redeemed |
| Is my night growing? | Each date of a repeating event against the ones before |
| What should I post next? | People tapping an activity on a day when nothing was on |

## Needs care, so not at launch

- **New versus returning visitors.** It means comparing people's perk
  records across events, and a small number could point at a person.
- **How far people came from.** It is derived from location, which the app
  promises stays on the phone.
- **Comparing against similar venues.** Only once there are enough venues
  that nobody can work out a competitor's numbers.

## Never

- Who viewed or attended: names, emails, or lists of any kind
- Age, gender, or orientation of visitors
- "Someone is on their way" alerts
- Where else a venue's customers go
- Letting venues message or advertise to visitors, or passing audiences to
  ad networks
- Exact times

For this audience, a list of who went where can out someone.

## Known limits

- The numbers can be inflated by someone determined to. App Check, on the
  launch list, makes that much harder. It matters most once hosts pay based
  on these numbers.
- Early numbers will be small.
