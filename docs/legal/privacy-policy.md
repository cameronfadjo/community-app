## The short version

- You can browse events without an account. If you do, we do not collect your name or email.
- An account is only needed to unlock perks. It needs a name, an email, and a password.
- Your location is used on your phone while the app is open. We do not save it and it is not sent to our servers.

## Who the app is for

The app is for people aged 18 and over. It lists events at bars and other venues for adults, and some perks are alcoholic drinks. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has made an account, contact us at [contact email] and we will delete it.

### When you unlock a perk

We keep a record that includes:

- which event it was, and the venue name
- what the perk was
- the time you unlocked it
- the time you redeemed it, if you did

We keep this so each perk is used once per person. This record shows that you were at that venue at that time. We treat it with care:

- Venues and event organizers see totals only, such as how many perks were unlocked. They do not see who unlocked them.
- Other people using the app cannot see your perks.

### Information handled automatically

When the app talks to our servers, our service provider receives technical details such as your IP address and device type. This happens with any internet service. It is used to run and protect the service. We do not use it to build a profile of you.

## What we do not collect

- Your location. See the next section.
- Your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.
- Payment details. The app is free and has no purchases.
- An advertising ID.
- Your sexual orientation or gender identity. We never ask.

## Location

If you allow it, the app reads your location while the app is open. It uses it for two things:

1. To show events near you, sorted by distance.
2. To confirm you have arrived when you tap to unlock a perk. You need to be within about 150 metres of the venue.

The comparison happens on your phone. Your location is not sent to our servers and is not saved. The app never uses your location in the background.

You can say no. Browsing still works. You will not be able to unlock perks, since they need an arrival check. You can change your choice at any time in your phone's settings.

If you tap for directions, your phone's maps app opens. That app has its own privacy policy.

## Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules them on your phone, using events it has already loaded. The activities you follow and your reminder settings are saved on your phone only. They are not sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your phone's settings.
