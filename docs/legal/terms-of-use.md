## 5. Perks

- A perk is a free offer from the venue or organizer, not from us.
- To unlock a perk you need an account, and you need to be at the venue. The app checks this with your phone's location while the app is open.

- Do not try to unlock perks by faking your location or by other tricks. We may close accounts that do.
